# Strategic Intelligence OS

## Mission

The Strategic Intelligence OS is the layer between the creator, the product system, and the market.
It maintains three explicit models:

1. SELF MODEL: what the system can actually do, what it has evidence for, and where its limits are.
2. CREATOR MODEL: stable, non-sensitive working preferences, project goals, decision constraints, and the creator's own stated hypotheses.
3. MARKET MODEL: customer problems, competing categories, standards, demand signals, pricing evidence, regulatory/security constraints, and dated market observations.

## Core loop

Observe -> Model -> Challenge -> Build -> Measure -> Position -> Package -> Validate -> Sell -> Learn

Every market fact carries a source and observation date. Every capability carries evidence. Every creator preference is attributed to an explicit user statement or project record.

## Self-model

The system must maintain a capability ledger with:
- capability name;
- implementation reference;
- maturity: SPECIFIED / PROTOTYPE / VERIFIED / PRODUCTION;
- evidence references;
- known failure modes;
- dependencies;
- last verification date.

It must never describe a capability as production-ready merely because code exists.

## Creator model

The creator model is not psychological profiling. It is an operational model built from explicit project facts and stated preferences.
It records:
- active objectives;
- product hypotheses;
- constraints;
- preferred working mode;
- approved terminology;
- rejected assumptions;
- unresolved decisions.

Sensitive personal attributes are excluded unless separately required and authorized by the surrounding system.

## Market model

Market intelligence is time-bounded. A record contains:
- market claim;
- segment;
- customer problem;
- evidence/source;
- publication date;
- observation date;
- confidence;
- counter-evidence;
- implications for product positioning.

Search is not truth. A search result becomes a market fact only after source inspection and provenance capture.

## Decision engine

For every candidate product opportunity, calculate a traceable opportunity record from:
- problem severity;
- frequency;
- buyer clarity;
- willingness-to-pay evidence;
- competitive saturation;
- technical differentiation;
- defensibility;
- regulatory/security burden;
- evidence strength;
- time-to-MVP.

No overall 'best idea' score is emitted. The system presents the evidence and trade-offs so the creator can choose.

## Current market signal

As of September 2026, enterprise attention is moving toward agent governance, security, identity, authorization, observability and cost control. Gartner describes agentic AI governance/security/cost management as emerging priorities, while OWASP has formalized agentic-specific threats and mitigations. NIST is actively developing identity and authorization guidance for software and AI agents.

These are market signals, not proof of customer demand for this project. Customer validation remains a separate gate.

## Commercial connection

Strategic Intelligence OS feeds Idea-to-Sale OS.

Strategic Intelligence -> Opportunity Record -> Product Spec -> Action Gate -> Evidence -> Offer -> Human Approval -> Payment -> Entitlement

## Non-negotiable rules

- No invented customer demand.
- No fabricated competitor weaknesses.
- No fabricated benchmark.
- No hidden version drift.
- No market claim without provenance.
- No sale marked SOLD without settlement evidence.
- No irreversible financial or contractual action without required human approval.

## Definition of done

The system knows itself when its capability ledger is evidence-backed.
It understands the creator when the creator's explicit project constraints and goals are represented without unsupported psychological inference.
It understands the market when dated, sourced evidence is attached to each material market claim and competing interpretations are retained.
It is commercially useful when those three models jointly change what gets built, measured, positioned and sold.
