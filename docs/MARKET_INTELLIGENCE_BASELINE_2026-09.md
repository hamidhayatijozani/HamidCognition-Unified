# Market Intelligence Baseline — September 2026

## Scope

This baseline is a dated research snapshot for the productization of AI-agent governance and action authorization.

## Observations

1. Gartner reports that enterprise agent sprawl and governance are becoming material management problems. Gartner's April 2026 release states that only 13% of surveyed organizations believe they have the right AI-agent governance in place and projects very large agent estates in Fortune 500 enterprises by 2028.
2. OWASP's Agentic Security Initiative has moved beyond generic LLM security into agent-specific threat models, including tool misuse, identity/privilege abuse and agent supply-chain risks.
3. NIST published a 2026 concept paper focused specifically on identity and authorization for software and AI agents, indicating that agent identity is becoming a standards and infrastructure concern.
4. Current industry reporting also shows agents moving from conversational assistance toward actions such as communication, booking and transactions, increasing the importance of authorization and control boundaries.

## Interpretation boundary

These observations support the existence of a real problem category around agent governance, security and authorization. They do not establish market size, customer willingness to pay, competitive superiority, or product-market fit for HamidCognition.

## Validation required

The next market-validation records must come from actual prospective buyers, design partners, paid pilots, procurement conversations, or other independently recorded demand evidence.
