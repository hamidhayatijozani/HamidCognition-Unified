# State Transition Integrity (STI)

**Status:** RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Origin:** cross-line synthesis of HamidCognition research artifacts

## Thesis

A recurring structure across HamidCognition lines is not a particular model or domain, but the transition from one epistemic/behavioral state to another.

The proposed research object is **State Transition Integrity (STI)**:

> the degree to which a system's transition from state S_t to S_{t+1} remains traceable, evidence-aligned, reproducible, and semantically consistent.

This is a hypothesis, not an established scientific law.

## Unified transition

```
OBSERVATION
   ↓
REPRESENTATION
   ↓
STATE
   ↓
TRANSITION
   ↓
EVIDENCE ALIGNMENT
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
ACTION
   ↓
OUTCOME
   ↺
REPLAY / UPDATE
```

## Candidate dimensions

STI is initially decomposed into measurable dimensions rather than a single score:

- **Traceability (T):** can the transition be reconstructed from recorded artifacts?
- **Evidence alignment (E):** does the strength of the new state/claim remain within available evidence?
- **Transition stability (S):** does the transition persist under rerun and controlled perturbation?
- **Semantic consistency (C):** does the state retain the same operational meaning across versions/contexts?
- **Uncertainty integrity (U):** does missing or contradictory evidence remain represented rather than silently collapsed?

A composite score must not be used until the dimensions have independent validation.

## Falsification program

The hypothesis gains no support merely because the abstraction fits existing diagrams.

It should be attacked with:

1. Simple baselines that ignore explicit state transitions.
2. Synthetic systems where provenance is complete but the transition is wrong.
3. Systems with correct outcomes but corrupted lineage.
4. Semantic drift across versioned state schemas.
5. Common-mode corruption across multiple validators.
6. Missing, stale and contradictory evidence.
7. Reordered inputs and randomized seeds.
8. Adversarial transitions engineered to preserve surface behavior while breaking causal/epistemic lineage.

## Minimum empirical question

Does an explicit transition-integrity representation predict or detect failures that are not reliably detected by outcome-only metrics, confidence, or provenance-only metrics?

A positive result must be out-of-sample and compared against simpler baselines.
