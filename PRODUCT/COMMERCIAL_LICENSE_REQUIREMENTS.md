# Commercial License Requirements

## Purpose
This document defines the minimum commercial authorization required before delivering HamidCognition Action Gate to a paying customer.

## Required agreement
The customer must receive a separate written license or other legally valid authorization because the repository license does not grant general rights to reproduce, modify, distribute, publish, commercialize, or create derivative works.

## License scope to define
- customer legal entity;
- licensed product and exact version;
- delivery mode: SELF_HOSTED, MANAGED, or ENTERPRISE;
- permitted deployment count/environment;
- permitted users, agents, tenants, or throughput where applicable;
- integration rights;
- modification rights, if any;
- redistribution and sublicensing restrictions;
- support and maintenance scope;
- update/upgrade rights;
- term and renewal;
- confidentiality and security responsibilities;
- ownership of customer data and policy configuration;
- intellectual-property ownership;
- termination and post-termination handling;
- applicable law and dispute terms.

## Technical attachment
The commercial agreement should reference the exact product delivery manifest, release tag, commit, artifact checksum, acceptance result, and any customer-specific integration scope.

## Boundary
This is an engineering/commercial checklist, not legal advice. Counsel should review the final agreement for the governing jurisdiction and customer structure.
