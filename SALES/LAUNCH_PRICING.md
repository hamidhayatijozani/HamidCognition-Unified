# Launch Pricing Hypothesis

These are proposed launch prices for commercial testing, not claims about market-standard pricing.

## 1. Paid Pilot

**USD 15,000–25,000 fixed**

One protected tool or MCP server, one deployment environment, integration, acceptance testing, evidence package and 30-day delivery window.

Use the pilot to establish customer value and integration effort before negotiating a larger contract.

## 2. SELF_HOSTED

**Starting hypothesis: USD 30,000/year**

Includes the Action Gate runtime, deployment contract, release artifacts, acceptance suite, standard documentation and defined support.

Customer infrastructure and customer-specific integration are separate.

## 3. MANAGED

**Starting hypothesis: USD 4,000/month**

Includes operation of the service boundary, standard monitoring and defined support.

Hosting, data residency, integration complexity, traffic volume and SLA commitments can change the price.

## 4. ENTERPRISE

**Custom annual contract**

Use when the customer requires:
- multiple protected tools;
- complex identity integration;
- private networking;
- security review;
- dedicated support;
- custom retention;
- contractual SLA;
- deployment architecture;
- customer-specific acceptance controls.

## Pricing rule

Do not discount the pilot into free consulting.

The pilot is the product's proof mechanism. It should have a defined scope, acceptance criteria, delivery date and commercial conversion path.
