# Launch Pricing Hypothesis

These are proposed launch prices for commercial testing, not claims about market-standard pricing.

## 1. Paid Pilot

**USD $2,500 fixed / 30 days**

One protected HTTP/MCP tool, one controlled test environment, agreed acceptance cases, deployment assistance and a reproducible evidence package. Expanded scope requires a separate written quote.

Use the pilot to establish customer value and integration effort before negotiating a larger contract.

## 2. SELF_HOSTED

**UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS — DO NOT QUOTE AS CURRENT OFFER: USD 30,000/year**

Includes the Action Gate runtime, deployment contract, release artifacts, acceptance suite, standard documentation and defined support.

Customer infrastructure and customer-specific integration are separate.

## 3. MANAGED

**UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS — DO NOT QUOTE AS CURRENT OFFER: USD 4,000/month**

Includes operation of the service boundary, standard monitoring and defined support.

Hosting, data residency, integration complexity, traffic volume and SLA commitments can change the price.

## 4. ENTERPRISE

**Custom annual contract**

Use when the customer requires:
- multiple protected tools;
- complex identity integration;
- private networking;
- security review;
- dedicated support;
- custom retention;
- contractual SLA;
- deployment architecture;
- customer-specific acceptance controls.

## Pricing rule

The only current standard first-deal offer is the $2,500 fixed / 30-day bounded pilot. The annual self-hosted and monthly managed figures above are hypotheses, not market-validated prices or authorized default quotes. Do not present them externally until buyer evidence, delivery costs and support obligations justify a revised pricing decision.

Do not discount the pilot into free consulting. Keep its scope, acceptance criteria, delivery window and conversion path explicit.
