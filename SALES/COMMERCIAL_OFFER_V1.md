# Action Gate Commercial Offer v1

## Objective

Convert the existing one-tool proof-of-value concept into a concrete paid pilot that a security/platform buyer can approve without committing to a platform migration.

## ICP

Primary buyer:
- CISO / Head of AI Security
- Platform Security lead
- AI Platform engineering lead

Trigger:
- an AI agent can call a production or high-impact HTTP/MCP tool;
- the organization needs to prove that unauthorized actions are blocked at the execution boundary;
- the buyer wants evidence they can reproduce rather than a generic AI-safety claim.

## Product promise

**Protect one consequential agent tool in 30 days and prove the tested execution boundary with executable acceptance evidence.**

The promise is deliberately narrower than "secure your agents." No universal safety, compliance, or business-outcome guarantee is implied.

## Paid pilot

### Pilot price

**$2,500 USD fixed fee / 30 days**

Payment route: USDT can be accepted only through a configured and verified payment address/network. Do not represent manual wallet-account settlement as automatic on-chain verification.

### Included

- one HTTP or MCP protected tool;
- one customer test environment;
- policy configuration for agreed ALLOW / DENY / ASK / SANDBOX cases;
- deployment assistance;
- direct-bypass test;
- action/tenant tampering test;
- expired-authority test;
- replay test;
- restart/persistence test;
- reproducible acceptance evidence;
- final deployment recommendation.

### Explicit exclusions

- organization-wide deployment;
- legal/compliance certification;
- guaranteed prevention of every AI attack;
- guaranteed business correctness;
- unrestricted custom development;
- production SLA.

## Conversion

If the pilot passes the agreed acceptance suite, the customer chooses one of:

**SELF_HOSTED**
- customer operates the deployment;
- annual software/support license negotiated from deployment scope.

**MANAGED**
- HamidCognition operates the service boundary;
- recurring fee negotiated from traffic, integration and support scope.

**ENTERPRISE**
- negotiated integration, security review, support, deployment architecture and SLA.

Do not invent annual pricing before a real buyer has validated deployment scope.

## Risk reversal

The pilot is acceptance-driven.

Before payment, the customer and provider agree:
1. the protected tool;
2. the test environment;
3. the acceptance cases;
4. the evidence package to be delivered.

The pilot is successful only when the agreed acceptance suite passes in the customer's deployment.

## Buyer CTA

**Book a 30-minute technical scoping call.**

Required inputs:
- agent/runtime;
- target HTTP or MCP tool;
- consequence of an unauthorized call;
- deployment environment;
- technical owner.

## Sales qualification gate

Do not pursue a lead as a pilot unless at least three are true:

- production or production-like agent exists;
- agent has write or consequential tool access;
- security/platform owner is involved;
- a specific tool can be isolated for a pilot;
- unauthorized execution has material risk;
- customer can provide a test environment.
