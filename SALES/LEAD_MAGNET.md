# Free Agent Action Boundary Check

## Offer

Bring one harmless but representative agent tool call.

We will show the difference between:

**Agent → Tool**

and

**Agent → Authorization Boundary → Tool**

The check focuses on three concrete questions:

1. Can an unauthorized call reach the protected tool?
2. Can an approved authority be replayed?
3. Can the authorized action be changed after approval?

## Output

You receive a short evidence report containing the observed requests, responses and tested controls.

No claim is made beyond the paths actually tested.

## Commercial next step

If the result shows a meaningful control gap, the next step is a 30-day paid pilot around one customer-selected high-impact tool.
