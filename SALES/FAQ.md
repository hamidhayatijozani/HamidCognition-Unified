# HamidCognition Action Gate — Commercial FAQ

## What is Action Gate?
A pre-execution authorization and evidence boundary between an AI agent and a protected tool or action.

## What does it decide?
The control model supports ALLOW, DENY, ASK, and SANDBOX decisions.

## What does it bind?
Authorization can be bound to tenant, actor, session, and action context, with single-use execution authorization.

## Does it replace an AI security platform?
No. The commercial positioning is complementary: existing AI security, identity, governance, and monitoring systems can provide context while Action Gate provides an enforcement boundary immediately before execution.

## What happens when authorization cannot be established?
The design is fail-closed where required authorization/evidence cannot be established.

## Does it support MCP?
The commercial product includes an MCP enforcement boundary as well as HTTP enforcement.

## Who buys it?
AI platform teams, agent infrastructure teams, AI security/governance teams, enterprise automation teams, MCP/tool platforms, and organizations operating higher-consequence agent workflows.

## How is it deployed?
Commercial models include self-hosted, managed, and enterprise deployment.

## What is the pilot?
A narrowly scoped proof of value around one protected agent-to-tool execution path, including integration, acceptance criteria, evidence/replay, and an outcome report.

## Pricing
Indicative commercial structure: $50,000 pilot; $150,000–$300,000/year enterprise licensing; strategic/OEM engagements from $500,000+, scope dependent.

## What is not claimed?
Action Gate is not presented as universal AI safety, universal correctness, or automatic regulatory compliance. Those claims require separate evidence and contractual scope.

## What does the buyer receive?
A versioned runtime, deployment materials, integration/API contract, acceptance procedure, operations guidance, security boundary/threat model, and release/provenance evidence, subject to the agreed commercial scope.
