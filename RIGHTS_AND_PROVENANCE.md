## Evidence hierarchy

For provenance purposes, priority is given to:

1. immutable Git commit identifiers;
2. tagged releases and archived releases;
3. persistent archival identifiers such as DOI;
4. dated research records and experiment records;
5. repository documentation and source lineage;
6. informal descriptions or external summaries.

This hierarchy records provenance. It does not by itself determine legal ownership or resolve disputes of authorship.

## Important limitation

A public GitHub repository is useful evidence of the history of material, but it is not a magical anti-theft force field. Legal enforceability depends on jurisdiction, ownership, contracts, prior rights, and the specific material involved. For stronger evidentiary preservation, releases should be archived through a persistent repository such as Zenodo and assigned a DOI.
